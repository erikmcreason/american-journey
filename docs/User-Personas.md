# The American Journey User Personas

## Persona 1: Maria

Age: 29

Background:
Maria immigrated from Mexico and works two jobs while supporting her family.

Challenges:
- Limited English proficiency
- Difficulty understanding paperwork
- Limited financial literacy
- Little time available for learning

Goals:
- Improve English
- Obtain permanent residency
- Increase income
- Purchase a home

Success Looks Like:
Maria feels confident navigating American systems and has a clear pathway toward citizenship and financial stability.

---

## Persona 2: Ahmed

Age: 34

Background:
Ahmed has an engineering degree and recently immigrated to the United States.

Challenges:
- Professional networking
- Understanding American workplace culture
- Translating foreign credentials
- Career advancement

Goals:
- Obtain professional employment
- Build a professional network
- Become a citizen

Success Looks Like:
Ahmed secures a professional career and successfully integrates into his local community.

## Persona 3: Julia

Age: 38

Background:
Julia has been navigating the immigration system for years and has encountered confusion around paperwork, deadlines, and legal requirements.

Challenges:
- Administrative complexity
- Legal uncertainty
- Information overload

Goals:
- Stay compliant
- Avoid mistakes
- Complete immigration requirements successfully

Success Looks Like:
Julia clearly understands her next steps and avoids costly administrative errors.

## Persona 4: Carlos

Challenges:
