# The American Journey Epics

## Epic 1: User Account System

Allow users to create accounts, log in, manage profiles, and save progress.

---

## Epic 2: Journey Dashboard

Provide users with a central dashboard displaying progress, readiness scores, milestones, and recommended next actions.

---

## Epic 3: English & Communication

Provide structured English learning and communication development.

---

## Epic 4: Financial Literacy & Opportunity

Provide financial education and opportunity-focused learning.

---

## Epic 5: Citizenship Readiness

Prepare users for citizenship requirements and testing.

---

## Epic 6: Administrative Readiness

Help users navigate paperwork, deadlines, notices, and compliance requirements.

---

## Epic 7: Cultural Integration & Community

Help users integrate into American society while preserving cultural identity.

---

## Epic 8: Progress & Achievement System

Provide badges, milestones, readiness scores, and progress tracking.
