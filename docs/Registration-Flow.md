# Registration Flow

## Purpose

Collect enough information to personalize The American Journey without overwhelming the user.

---

## Step 1: Account Creation

Fields:

* First Name
* Last Name (Optional)
* Email Address
* Password

Button:

Create Account

---

## Step 2: Welcome Screen

Message:

Welcome to The American Journey.

We will help you navigate immigration, integration, citizenship preparation, and opportunity in America.

Button:

Begin Assessment

---

## Step 3: Immigration Profile

Questions:

What best describes your current status?

Options:

* Future Immigrant
* Visa Holder
* Permanent Resident
* Green Card Holder
* Refugee / Asylum Seeker
* Other

---

## Step 4: Country of Origin

Question:

What country are you coming from?

Purpose:

Personalization and future cultural content.

---

## Step 5: English Assessment

Question:

How comfortable are you speaking English?

Options:

* Beginner
* Basic
* Intermediate
* Advanced

Purpose:

Determine starting English track.

---

## Step 6: Financial Literacy Assessment

Question:

How familiar are you with the following?

* Banking
* Credit Scores
* Budgeting
* Taxes

Scale:

1-5

Purpose:

Determine starting financial literacy level.

---

## Step 7: Citizenship Readiness Assessment

Question:

How familiar are you with:

* U.S. Government
* Constitution
* U.S. History

Scale:

1-5

Purpose:

Determine citizenship readiness level.

---

## Step 8: Goals Selection

What are your primary goals?

Select all that apply:

* Learn English
* Become a Citizen
* Find Employment
* Start a Business
* Buy a Home
* Improve Finances
* Understand American Culture
* Help My Family Succeed

---

## Step 9: Results Screen

Display:

* Readiness Score
* Recommended Starting Track
* Recommended Next Action

Button:

Go To Dashboard
