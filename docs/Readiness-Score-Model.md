# Readiness Score Model

## Purpose

The Readiness Score provides users with a simple measurement of their overall preparedness for successful integration, opportunity, and citizenship in America.

The score serves as:

* A progress indicator
* A motivational tool
* A recommendation engine
* A readiness benchmark

---

# Overall Readiness Score

Range:

0 - 100

The score is calculated from five core categories.

---

## Category 1: English & Communication

Weight: 25%

Measures:

* English proficiency
* Lesson completion
* Assessment scores

---

## Category 2: Financial Literacy & Opportunity

Weight: 20%

Measures:

* Banking knowledge
* Budgeting knowledge
* Credit understanding
* Financial lesson completion

---

## Category 3: Citizenship Readiness

Weight: 20%

Measures:

* Constitution knowledge
* Government knowledge
* History knowledge
* Citizenship assessment scores

---

## Category 4: Administrative Readiness

Weight: 20%

Measures:

* Paperwork knowledge
* Deadline awareness
* Scam awareness
* Process understanding

---

## Category 5: Cultural Integration & Community

Weight: 15%

Measures:

* Community participation
* Cultural knowledge
* Integration lessons
* Volunteer activities

---

# Readiness Levels

## Explorer

0-20

Beginning the journey.

---

## Builder

21-40

Building foundational skills.

---

## Contributor

41-60

Developing practical readiness.

---

## Pathfinder

61-80

Demonstrating strong integration readiness.

---

## Ambassador

81-100

Demonstrating exceptional preparedness and community contribution.
